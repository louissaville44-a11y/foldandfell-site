Painting & decorating in Burley-in-Wharfedale

Burley mixes stone terraces and cottages around Main Street with newer family homes on the edges of the village. Whether it is a full interior repaint, a hallway and stairs, or refreshed woodwork, we plan the work around family life and keep disruption to a minimum.

Many older Burley homes still have original sash windows, panelled doors and stone exteriors. We repair, prepare and repaint them with materials suited to period properties.
