We paint and decorate homes across Wharfedale with patience, care and proper preparation, so the finish looks right and lasts for years.

A considered approach to decorating.

Before founding Fold & Fell, Louis Saville spent his career in graphic and digital design, developing an appreciation for colour, balance, composition and the finer details that often go unnoticed.

It was this sensitivity to design that became the foundation for Fold & Fell: a painting and decorating practice rooted in Wharfedale, bringing together an eye for aesthetics with a respect for traditional craftsmanship.

For Louis, decorating is about more than applying paint. It’s about understanding a space, respecting its character and recognising how subtle choices in colour, texture and finish can change the way a room feels. Fold & Fell was created around this philosophy, offering a personal service for those who appreciate thoughtful design, careful workmanship and lasting quality.
